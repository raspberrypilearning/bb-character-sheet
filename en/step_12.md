## Challenge

Choose one or more ways to make your Brainrot Brigade character sheet your own.

> [!CHALLENGE]
>
> + Can you ask whether a roll uses Logic or Instinct, then count how many dice succeed? Remember: Logic succeeds at or below the core number, while Instinct succeeds at or above it.
> + Can you add labels or an instruction panel that explains the P, E, T, B, and space-key shortcuts?
> + Can you add another avatar layer for headgear, an accessory, or a sidekick, then make it respond to clicks?
> + Can you add a loot list for rewards collected during adventures without mixing those rewards into the three starting gear items?
> + Can you add your own style, role, name, and concept ideas to the hidden lists so the random generator makes characters nobody else has seen?
> + Can you make a random loadout generator that follows the rule of no more than one permanent starting item?

> [!SAVE]
